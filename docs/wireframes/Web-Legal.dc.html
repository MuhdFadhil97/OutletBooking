Privacy notice

Last updated [date] · FTech IT Consulting Sdn Bhd [SSM no.]

This notice explains how FTech IT Consulting Sdn Bhd, the company behind OutletBooking, collects and uses personal data, as required by the Personal Data Protection Act 2010.

1. Who this notice covers

  • Business owners and staff — we decide how your account data is used.
  • Customers booking through a business’s page — the business decides; we store and process it on the business’s behalf.
  • Website visitors who contact us — we decide.

2. Personal data we collect

  • Account: name, email, mobile, password (hashed), business details, encrypted ToyyibPay key.
  • Bookings: customer name, mobile, answers to booking questions, booking details and payment status.
  • We never receive card numbers or online banking logins.

Refund policy

In short

  • Plans are paid in advance; no refunds for unused days.
  • Charged twice, or paid but not applied: full refund within 14 working days.
  • Customer booking refunds are handled by the business — OutletBooking never holds that money.
