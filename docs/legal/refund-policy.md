## 1. What this policy covers
This policy covers **plan fees** that businesses pay FTech IT Consulting Sdn Bhd for OutletBooking.

It does **not** cover payments customers make to a business for a booking. Those go directly to the business's own ToyyibPay account; the business sets and handles its own cancellation and refund rules. Customers should contact the business.

## 2. Free trial
The 7-day free trial costs nothing, so there is nothing to refund. You are never charged automatically at the end of the trial.

## 3. Plan fees
- Plans are paid in advance. **We don't give refunds for unused days** in a period you have paid for, including if you stop using the service part-way through.
- If you stop paying, your plan simply runs until the end of the period you have paid for.
- **First payment guarantee [suggested]:** if you are not happy within **[7] days** of your first paid plan, ask us and we will refund that first payment in full.

## 4. When we always refund
We refund in full when:
- you were **charged twice** for the same period;
- money left your account but the payment **failed or was not applied** to your plan, and we can't apply it;
- we **close the service** or end your account without you being at fault — we refund the unused part of the period you paid for.

## 5. Changing plans
- **Upgrade:** [you pay the difference for the rest of the current period / the new price applies from your next payment] — **[decide]**.
- **Downgrade:** the lower price applies from your next payment. No partial refund for the current period.

## 6. How to ask for a refund
Contact us within **[30] days** of the payment at [billing@outletbooking.my] or WhatsApp [+60 …] with:
- your business name and registered email;
- the receipt number (for example OB-2026-000123) or ToyyibPay reference;
- the reason.

We reply within [3] working days. Approved refunds are paid by bank transfer to the business's bank account within **[14] working days**. We can't refund to a different person or company.

## 7. Payment not completed
If ToyyibPay shows "payment not completed" but money left your bank account, don't pay again. Usually your bank returns it automatically; if it doesn't, contact us with the ToyyibPay reference and we will check with ToyyibPay.
